##### Detailed study of the components involved:<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 1.  Form Panel:<br>
A "form panel" in the PERI column formwork system is a flat panel used to mould the shape of concrete columns during construction. Form panels serve the following purpose.<br>

- Shaping: Defines the exact dimensions and shape of the concrete column.<br>
- Support: Holds wet concrete in place until it sets.<br>
- Finish: Provides a smooth surface for a clean column finish.<br>
- Modularity: Allows for easy assembly, adjustment, and reuse.<br>
- Durability: Withstands the pressure of curing concrete, ensuring structural integrity.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 2.  Form Corner:<br>
A "form corner" in the PERI column formwork system is a specialized component used to create and support the corners of concrete columns. Form Corner serves the following purpose.<br>

- Corner Shaping: Defines and maintains the precise corner angles of the column.<br>
- Support: Provides structural stability and reinforcement at the corners.<br>
- Alignment: Ensures accurate positioning and alignment of the formwork panels.<br>
- Durability: Withstands the pressure of the concrete, preventing deformation and ensuring a clean corner finish.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 3. Push Prop:<br>
Push props" in the PERI column formwork system are adjustable vertical supports used to stabilize and align formwork components. Push Props serves the following purpose.<br>

- Stabilization: Provides additional support to keep formwork panels securely in place.<br>
- Alignment: Helps maintain precise vertical alignment of formwork elements during setup.<br>
- Adjustability: Allows for fine-tuning of formwork positions to ensure correct dimensions and stability.<br>
- Load Distribution: Assists in distributing the load from the concrete evenly, preventing formwork failure.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 4. Brace Kicker:<br>
A "brace kicker" in the PERI column formwork system is a diagonal support used to reinforce the formwork structure. Brace Kickers serves the following purpose.<br>

- Stability: Provides diagonal bracing to prevent formwork panels from shifting or collapsing.<br>
- Alignment: Ensures that the formwork remains in the correct position during concrete pouring.<br>
- Load Transfer: Helps transfer loads from the formwork to the supporting structure, maintaining formwork integrity.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 5. Wedge Pin:<br>
A "wedge pin" in the PERI column formwork system is a fastening component used to secure and adjust formwork panels and other elements. Wedge Pins serves the following purpose.<br>

- Secure Fastening: Locks formwork panels and components in place to prevent movement.<br>
- Adjustment: Allows for precise alignment and adjustments of formwork positions.<br>
- Stability: Enhances the overall stability of the formwork system during concrete pouring and curing.<br>
</div>
